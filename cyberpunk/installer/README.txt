CyberSkate - Skate 3 in Cyberpunk 2077

1. Install RED4ext and Cyber Engine Tweaks if you have not already.
2. Run Install-CyberSkate.bat. It finds your game, copies the mod into it
   and asks for your Skate 3 default.xex (keep its data folder beside it).
3. In game: CET overlay > Bindings > CyberSkate > Toggle skateboard, or
   click both sticks in at the same time.

Details: docs/CYBERPUNK.md in https://github.com/DoNotPetMe/CyberSkate
